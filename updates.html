Version history

1.2.1

24 September 2026 Latest
  • Forms are filled precisely, or not at all. When a PDF declares its own fields, ProcVault fills them exactly where the form says each value belongs. It no longer marks competency boxes on forms whose layout it cannot read reliably — a mark in the wrong column on a real credentialing document is worse than no mark at all.
  • Competency elements confirmed before signing. The supervisor ticks the standard elements — indication, consent, patient identifiers, landmarks, sterile technique, no complication, placement confirmed, documentation. Nothing is pre-checked, and an element left unticked is simply absent from the record rather than recorded as a failure.
  • Self-attestations are labelled as such. Signing your own procedure is recorded as a self-attestation, on the entry and on the exported PDF. "I did this" and "I watched them do this" are different claims, and a credentialing committee should be able to tell which one it is reading.
  • Sideways signatures can be turned upright before they are saved.

1.2

26 August 2026
  • Review & Sign: reading a form's own checklist items so a supervisor could confirm them in the app. Replaced in 1.2.1 — it could not place marks reliably across the variety of real hospital forms.
  • Marks placed in measured table cells rather than estimated positions.
  • On-device intelligence to separate competency items from headers and instructions.

1.1

14 August 2026
  • Signatures are cropped to the ink, so they fill the box they are shown in instead of rendering small and off-centre.
  • Document vault: keep completed, signed forms and produce them at the next site.
  • Dashboard figures are tappable through to the entries behind them.

1.0

7 August 2026
  • First release. Two-tap logging, supervisor signatures captured at the bedside, attestation PDFs, hospital form filling, PHI-safe image attachments, and credentialing targets — all on-device, with no account and no data collection.

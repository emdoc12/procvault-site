ProcVault Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

The short version

ProcVault does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything you enter

stays on your device.

What ProcVault stores

  • Procedure log entries you create (procedure type, date, role, ultrasound

    • guidance, optional site and notes)

    • Optional supervisor names and supervisor signatures you capture on an

      • entry, with the time of signing

      • Optional images you attach to entries
        • Optional credentialing targets and your display name for PDF exports

        All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.

        ProcVault has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

        Nothing you enter ever leaves your device unless you explicitly export or

        share it yourself.

        Images and protected health information

        ProcVault is designed so that no patient identifiers should ever enter the

        app:

        • Entry fields are intended for counts and technique details only, and the

          • app reminds you not to enter patient identifiers.

          • When you attach an image, ProcVault scans it **on your device** using

            • Apple's Vision framework and proposes black-box redactions over all

            detected text, plus the banner region where imaging machines typically

            burn in patient data. You review and confirm the redactions before

            anything is saved.

            • Only the redacted copy is stored. The original photo is never saved by

              • ProcVault, and image metadata (EXIF, location) is stripped.

              Automatic text detection is best-effort. **You are responsible for

              verifying that no protected health information remains** in images you

              save, export, or share, and for complying with your institution's policies

              and applicable law (including HIPAA).

              Photo library access

              ProcVault uses Apple's out-of-process photo picker. The app can only access

              the specific photos you select — never your full library.

              Purchases

              ProcVault Pro purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App

              Store. ProcVault receives only an anonymous entitlement status and never

              sees your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to the

              transaction: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

              Exports and sharing

              When you generate a PDF or CSV, or share an image, the file goes wherever

              you choose to send it via the iOS share sheet (printer, email, files, etc.).

              That transfer is under your control and outside ProcVault's storage.

              Data deletion

              Deleting an entry or image removes it from the app's storage. Deleting the

              app removes all ProcVault data from your device. There is no server-side

              copy to delete.

              Changes to this policy

              If a future version of ProcVault changes any of the above (for example,

              optional iCloud sync), this policy will be updated and the change will be

              noted in the App Store release notes before it takes effect.
